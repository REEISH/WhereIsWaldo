# Hidden Character Hunt

A classic "Where's Waldo" style photo-tagging game spanning three wildly different universes. Race against the clock to locate specific characters hidden within crowded, highly detailed scenes.

##  Live Demo

**Play the game here:** [https://zesty-pie-2e0c9e.netlify.app/](https://zesty-pie-2e0c9e.netlify.app/)

## Features

* **Three Unique Themes:**
  * 🧝 **LOTR (Lord of the Rings):** Search for the Trio during the Battle of Minas Tirth.
  * 🚁 **Doraemon:** Find the futuristic robotic cat and his friends.
  * 🎈 **IT:** A spooky hunt for Pennywise and members of the Losers' Club. 
* **Interactive Targeting:** Click anywhere on the image to open a targeting reticle and select the character you've found.
* **Real-time Validation:** Instant feedback on whether your coordinate guess was a hit or a miss.
* **Speed Timer:** Tracks exactly how long it takes you to find all characters on a given level.


##  How to Play

1. Open the [live demo](https://zesty-pie-2e0c9e.netlify.app/).
2. Select your desired level (LOTR, Doraemon, or IT) from the main menu.
3. Scan the image for the target characters.
4. Click directly on a character when you spot them and select their name from the dropdown menu.
5. Find all required characters as fast as possible to complete the level.
